What a Principal Solution Owner does

Not a generalist consultant. Not a product manager for hire. A practitioner who ships.

I take a complex operational problem and deliver a working, integrated solution. Designed, built, tested against real data, and handed back so your team owns it. The value is the process and the practitioner, not the sector.

Clients in charity, catering, and life sciences buy the same thing: someone who can translate a messy problem into something that runs. Low-code where it fits, AI-enabled where it earns its place, always built to be maintained by the people who use it.

Life sciences · EDC access
30 minDown from days per request, across 208 ICON-owned systems.

Getting into a live trial system took days. The trial didn’t.

Access to ICON’s EDC systems ran through a manual queue. Every request meant someone checking a role and assigning it by hand, then hoping nothing was mistyped. Across 208 systems, that queue was the bottleneck, not the trial.

I led the initiative as ICON’s embedded Solution Owner, connecting the EDC vendor’s API straight into ServiceNow, ICON’s own workflow platform. Requests now validate and route themselves, a two-stage approval keeps oversight in place, and a self-service portal replaced the ticket queue entirely.
